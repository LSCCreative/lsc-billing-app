Pricing & Budgeting
Q: How much does a video project cost?
A: Costs vary depending on project scale, deliverables, and production value. Simple single-camera interviews, event coverage or social media clips typically start between $1,000 and $3,500, while multi-day brand films, commercial shoots, or extensive editing generally range from $5,000 to $25,000+. Every project receives a custom line-item quote based on your specific requirements.
Q: What factors influence the cost of a video?
A: Primary cost drivers include:
Days & Crew Size: Number of shoot days, camera operators, sound engineers, gaffers, and directors.
Talent & Locations: Paid actors, voiceover artists, studio rentals, and location permits.
Post-Production: Complexity of motion graphics, visual effects, custom sound design, and colour grading.
Deliverables: The total number of cut-downs and aspect ratios requested (e.g., 16:9 for YouTube vs. 9:16 for TikTok).
Process & Timelines
Q: How long does the production process take?
A: Standard corporate or promotional projects take 3 to 8 weeks from initial kickoff to final delivery:
Pre-Production (1–2 weeks): Strategy, scriptwriting, storyboarding, and scheduling.
Production (1–5 days): Filming and audio capture.
Post-Production (2–3 weeks): Editing, colour grading, sound mixing, and client revision rounds.
Q: What are the main steps in your workflow?
A: Projects move through three clear phases:
Pre-Production: Align on goals, write scripts, plan camera and shot technique, cast talent, and lock in the shoot schedule.
Production: Arrive on location with cinema-grade cameras, audio, and lighting gear to capture all required footage.
Post-Production: We edit the visual cut, add music, sound effects, motion graphics, and colour grade, delivering drafts through an online platform for your team's feedback.
Creative & Client Involvement
Q: Do I need to provide a script or visual concept?
A: No. If you have a fully formed concept, we will bring it to life. If you only have a high-level business objective, we will handle concept development, messaging structure, and scriptwriting from scratch.
Q: How involved does my team need to be?
A: Your heaviest involvement happens during pre-production to approve creative direction, scripts, and schedules. During post-production, we ask for feedback at specific milestone cuts (First Cut and Fine Cut). On filming days, a team member is welcome to accompany the crew on set, but we manage all logistics.
Q: How many rounds of edits are included?
A: Standard packages include two rounds of revisions (First Cut for structure/pacing and Fine Cut for minor tweaks). Revisions are submitted via an interactive online tool where you can pin comments directly to exact timestamps on the video frame.
Licensing & Technical Deliverables
Q: What video formats and aspect ratios will I receive?
A: Final files are exported in high-bitrate 4K or 1080p .mp4 format, optimised for web, television, and social media. We provide cut-downs built for specific channels:
16:9 (Horizontal): Websites, YouTube, LinkedIn, TV.
9:16 (Vertical): Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
1:1 (Square): Instagram Feed and paid social ad placements.
Q: Who owns the copyright and the raw footage?
A: Upon final payment, your company receives full licensing rights to host, distribute, and broadcast the final edited video indefinitely. Raw camera footage (unedited clips) remains the property of this production business, but can be purchased as an add-on alongside a hard drive delivery fee.
